# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in AgentHawk welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, assume good intent while evaluating evidence, give actionable feedback, accept responsibility for mistakes, and prioritize the health and safety of the community.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, unwanted sexual attention, deliberate intimidation, publishing private information, and sustained disruption are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile contact options. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
